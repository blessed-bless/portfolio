## Problem

The cafe had a basic site. It needed one page with the menu, photos, and how to get there. I rebuilt it by hand, with my own components. This is not the cafe's official site.

## What I built

- **Menu.** Tabs for the main menu and drinks. Inside each, a price filter: 200, 400, and other prices.
- **Gallery.** A photo grid. A tap opens a lightbox with the picture, a caption, and back, next, and close.
- **Map and contacts.** Anapa, Gorky Street 6. Open daily 9:00–23:00. The phone number is on the page.
- **Phone bar.** Below 800px a bar stays at the bottom: call and directions. It is hidden on a wide screen.
- **Scroll.** The browser's own scroll, no scroll-jacking.
- **Logo.** Sofia Sans Condensed, the font file hosted with the page.
- **Images.** AVIF and WebP.
