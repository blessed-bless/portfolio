## Problem

Show that I also ship sites on Tilda — for clients who need to edit copy, photos, and the menu without a developer. Not “constructor for its own sake”, but a working storefront with brand style and real flows: menu, seasonal offer, pre-order, map.

## Solution

- **Tilda + Zero / HTML blocks.** Custom layout where templates fall short; content stays editable in the editor.
- **Brand system.** Sea-buckthorn accent `#E8892A`, fonts Oswald / Caveat / Manrope.
- **Card menu with filters.** Classics, alternative, non-coffee, food — no endless price list.
- **Seasonal offer** on its own screen, pre-order with personal-data consent, map, and 320–1440 responsive layout.
