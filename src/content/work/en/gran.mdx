## Problem

The shop had no website. Clients booked by phone; prices and barbers lived in chat threads. They needed a storefront where booking starts immediately — and a clear bridge to the barber’s day in Telegram.

## Solution

- **Three-step booking on the first screen.** Master → service → slot. The card sits in the hero, not on a separate page.
- **Masters, before/after, reviews.** Portraits with work previews on hover/tap, compare sliders, review strip.
- **Owner screen.** Short pitch: the site is the storefront; bookings go to the Smena Mini App; windows sync with YCLIENTS.
- **Paired with Smena.** The request reaches the barber in Telegram — this case is the client-facing front for that loop.
