## Problem

One stack from a site lead to a human: `/api/chat`, a Telegram bot that qualifies, a mini-CRM (filters, notes, timeline, soft-delete, CSV), and outbound webhook / notify that survives process crashes.

## Approach

FastAPI owns the API and outbox. SQLAlchemy + SQLite keep the CRM state. aiogram runs the bot. An outbox worker drains notify jobs so a crash mid-send does not drop the lead.
