## Problem

A small tire shop loses calls in peak season: the tech is under the car and cannot pick up. Paper notes, phone bookings, and walk-ins collide. The customer has no idea when they will be seen.

Reception covers the path from a missed call to a confirmed web booking for a standard service — without hiring a front-desk person or standing up a full CRM on day one.

## Decisions

- **SMS with a booking link.** On the first matching miss, the customer gets an SMS with the shop name and a mobile form link. A repeat from the same number to the same shop within 24 hours does not send another SMS.
- **Shared calendar.** Slots live in one place so phone, web, and walk-in traffic stop double-booking.
- **Telegram to the tech.** Confirmed bookings notify the person who actually does the work.
